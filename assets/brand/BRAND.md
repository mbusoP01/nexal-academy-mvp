# Nexal Pathway brand

Parent brand: Nexal
Product: Nexal Pathway
Tagline: Learn with direction.
Usage: Nexal Pathway by Nexal

Nexal Pathway describes a clear, progressive route through secondary learning.
The mark is an open portal with a rising pathway: structured study, visible
progress and room to grow. Oxford blue is the trusted academic foundation,
emerald is active learning, and gold is the signal for insight and achievement.

Tone: confident, clear, encouraging, calm, evidence-led.
